# Evolution Hub AI Layer
## Version 1.0.0-alpha

---

# Purpose

The AI Layer is the intelligence engine of Evolution Hub.

It provides conversational AI, business reasoning, retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), intelligent agents, workflow intelligence, and contextual decision support across the entire platform.

---

# AI Philosophy

AI should assist.

AI should automate.

AI should explain.

AI should never confuse the user.
