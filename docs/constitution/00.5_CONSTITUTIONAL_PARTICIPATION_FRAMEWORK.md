This Constitutional Book establishes how

==========================================================
PART I

CONSTITUTIONAL PARTICIPATION PHILOSOPHY
==========================================================

Evolution Hub is a constitutional
civilization built upon opportunity,
growth, collaboration, stewardship, and
earned progression.

Participation within the platform is
voluntary and progressive.

Every participant is welcomed according
to their purpose and intended interaction
with the ecosystem.

Participation shall not automatically
grant constitutional membership.

Commerce shall not automatically grant
citizenship.

Membership shall be earned through
constitutional commitment and continued
participation within the Evolution Hub
Civilization.

The platform shall encourage growth by
expanding opportunity rather than
restricting participation.

Progression shall always unlock broader
capabilities while preserving legitimate
capabilities already possessed.

Evolution Hub recognizes that different
participants contribute differently to
the ecosystem.

Some discover.

Some consume.

Some build.

All contribute to the continued evolution
of the platform according to the
Constitution.

==========================================================
CONSTITUTIONAL PARTICIPATION PRINCIPLES
==========================================================

• Participation does not imply Membership.

• Commerce does not imply Citizenship.

• Membership expands opportunity.

• Progression shall increase legitimate
  capability.

• Trust precedes commerce.

• Stewardship precedes authority.

• The Constitution remains supreme over
  every participant.

PART II

CONSTITUTIONAL PARTICIPANT CLASSES
==========================================================

PURPOSE

The Evolution Hub Operating System
recognizes multiple constitutional
participant classes.

Each class serves a distinct purpose
within the ecosystem.

Participation shall be progressive.

No participant shall automatically obtain
the rights, capabilities, or
responsibilities of another class unless
constitutionally defined.

The Platform shall recognize the
following constitutional participant
classes.

----------------------------------------------------------
1. VISITOR
----------------------------------------------------------

A Visitor is an individual who enters the
Evolution Hub ecosystem for discovery.

Visitors may freely explore the platform,
learn about its opportunities, and become
familiar with the Evolution Hub
Civilization.

Visitors are participants in discovery,
not constitutional members of the
civilization.

----------------------------------------------------------
2. CUSTOMER
----------------------------------------------------------

A Customer is a participant who engages
the Evolution Hub ecosystem through
commerce.

Customers may purchase products,
subscribe to services, communicate with
authorized sellers, and consume value
created within the ecosystem.

Customers participate in commerce but do
not automatically become constitutional
members of the Evolution Hub
Civilization.

----------------------------------------------------------
3. EVOLUTIONARIAN MEMBER
----------------------------------------------------------

An Evolutionarian Member is a
constitutionally recognized participant
who joins the Evolution Hub Civilization.

Members contribute to the growth of the
ecosystem through collaboration,
innovation, stewardship, and earned
progression.

Members may participate in constitutional
systems including Teams, Workspaces,
Projects, Opportunities, Industries,
Capacity, and other constitutional
capabilities.

Membership represents participation in
the civilization rather than mere use of
the platform.

==========================================================
CONSTITUTIONAL CLASSIFICATION PRINCIPLE
==========================================================

The Evolution Hub Platform shall always
distinguish between:

Discovery

Commerce

Civilization

Every participant shall be constitutionally
recognized according to their purpose
within the ecosystem.

Participation shall always respect the
constitutional identity of each class.

  Movements.

  capabilities according to earned
  eligibility.

==========================================================
