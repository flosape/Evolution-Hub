CONSTITUTIONAL ALIGNMENT

==========================================================

PURPOSE

The Service Allocation Constitution
defines the constitutional ownership,
placement, and responsibility of every
Evolution Hub Operating System service.

Its purpose is to ensure that every service
belongs to the correct constitutional
office and operates within clearly defined
boundaries.

==========================================================

CONSTITUTIONAL PRINCIPLE

Every service must belong to a defined
constitutional office.

Every service must have:

• A responsible office.

• A defined purpose.

• Clear capabilities.

• Clear liabilities.

• Constitutional boundaries.

==========================================================

SERVICE ALLOCATION RULE

No service shall exist without:

1. Constitutional Purpose.

2. Responsible Office.

3. Defined Authority Boundary.

4. Capability Definition.

5. Liability Definition.

==========================================================

OFFICE OWNERSHIP PRINCIPLE

An office is responsible for the services
assigned to it.

Responsibility includes:

• Service direction.

• Service alignment.

• Service accountability.

• Service integrity.

==========================================================

SERVICE LIMITATION PRINCIPLE

A service shall not:

• Create constitutional authority.

• Bypass its responsible office.

• Assume another service's responsibility.

• Operate outside constitutional limits.

==========================================================

SERVICE ALLOCATION STRUCTURE

Constitution

        ↓

Platform Offices

        ↓

Services

        ↓

Repositories

        ↓

Database

        ↓

Implementation

==========================================================

SERVICE CLASSIFICATION

Every service shall be classified by:

• Office ownership.

• Service purpose.

• User interaction.

• Data responsibility.

• Operational dependency.
