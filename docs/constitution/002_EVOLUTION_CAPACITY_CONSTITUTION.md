CAPACITY REVIEW
==========================================================

Capacity Review is the constitutional
process through which Evolution Capacity
decisions are examined to ensure fairness,
integrity, and alignment with the EHOS
Constitution.

Review exists to protect the trust placed
in the Capacity system.

==========================================================

REVIEW PURPOSE

Capacity Review ensures:

• Progression decisions remain fair.

• Capability unlocking remains justified.

• Evolution Stars remain trustworthy.

• Capacity boundaries remain protected.

• Participant growth remains constitutional.

==========================================================

RESPONSIBILITIES

Evolution Capacity is responsible for:

• Reviewing progression standards.

• Reviewing capability eligibility.

• Reviewing Evolution Star integrity.

• Maintaining transparent advancement
  principles.

• Identifying Capacity system failures.

• Preserving historical progression records.

==========================================================

CAPABILITIES

Evolution Capacity has the capability to:

• Conduct Capacity assessments.

• Review contribution records.

• Evaluate progression accuracy.

• Identify incorrect capability assignments.

• Recommend corrections.

• Improve Capacity processes while
  preserving constitutional principles.

==========================================================

LIABILITIES

Evolution Capacity shall not:

• Use review to create governance power.

• Remove participant protections.

• Change constitutional principles.

• Override Platform Governance.

• Convert review authority into operational
  authority.

• Use Capacity Review as punishment without
  constitutional basis.

==========================================================

CONSTITUTIONAL CONSTRAINT

Capacity Review protects fairness.

It does not create hierarchy.

It does not determine human worth.

It exists to ensure that earned capability
remains trustworthy, transparent, and
constitutional.
