1.9

CONSTITUTIONAL REVIEW
==========================================================

Constitutional Review is the formal process
through which Platform Governance evaluates
whether Evolution Hub development remains
aligned with constitutional authority,
responsibility boundaries, and operating
principles.

Constitutional Review protects EHOS from
structural drift during continuous evolution.

==========================================================

RESPONSIBILITIES

Platform Governance shall:

• Review constitutional documents before
  activation.

• Review proposed structural changes.

• Evaluate alignment with Book 0.

• Confirm responsibility ownership.

• Identify authority conflicts.

• Preserve constitutional consistency.

• Maintain constitutional review records.

==========================================================

REVIEW AREAS

Constitutional Review shall evaluate:

1. Authority Alignment

Does the proposed action belong to the
correct constitutional authority?

2. Responsibility Alignment

Is the responsibility assigned to the
correct Office, Service, or Repository?

3. Capability Alignment

Does the capability exceed its constitutional
boundary?

4. Liability Alignment

Does the structure preserve accountability
and limitations?

5. Evolution Alignment

Does the change preserve the long-term
vision and integrity of EHOS?

==========================================================

CAPABILITIES

Platform Governance may:

• Conduct constitutional assessments.

• Issue review findings.

• Recommend alignment changes.

• Request clarification.

• Escalate unresolved constitutional
  conflicts.

• Approve governance alignment where
  authority permits.

==========================================================

LIABILITIES

Platform Governance shall not:

• Block lawful evolution without
  constitutional justification.

• Replace another Office's responsibility.

• Make operational decisions.

• Determine commercial success.

• Determine participant worth.

• Modify systems directly.

==========================================================

CONSTITUTIONAL CONSTRAINT

Constitutional Review protects the path of
Evolution.

It does not stop evolution.

It ensures evolution occurs within the
constitutional order established by EHOS.

1.10

RATIFICATION
==========================================================

The Platform Governance Constitution is
hereby constitutionally ratified as the
authoritative governance framework of the
Evolution Hub Operating System.

This Constitution derives its authority from:

• EHOS Constitutional Execution Framework
  (Constitution Book 0).

• The Constitutional Authority Hierarchy.

• The Separation of Responsibilities
  Principle.

• The Rule of Constitution.

Platform Governance shall operate only
within the authority granted by the
Constitution.

Its purpose shall remain:

• Constitutional interpretation.

• Constitutional protection.

• Constitutional compliance.

• Constitutional review.

==========================================================

FINAL GOVERNANCE BOUNDARY

Platform Governance:

MAY

• Interpret constitutional principles.

• Review constitutional alignment.

• Protect constitutional boundaries.

• Provide governance guidance.

• Recommend constitutional amendments.

----------------------------------------------------------

SHALL NOT

• Create constitutional law independently.

• Exercise override authority.

• Determine Evolution Capacity.

• Grant earned capabilities.

• Execute business operations.

• Control repositories or databases.

• Replace constitutional offices.

==========================================================

FINAL CONSTITUTIONAL STATEMENT

Governance exists to protect the
Constitution.

Governance does not become the Constitution.

Authority remains separated.

Responsibilities remain assigned.

Capabilities remain bounded.

Liabilities remain accountable.

The Constitution remains supreme.
