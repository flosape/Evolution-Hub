CONSTITUTIONAL PURPOSE
==========================================================

The Team Membership Repository exists to
constitutionally preserve the lawful
membership relationship between Members and
Teams.

Its purpose is to provide a trusted
constitutional persistence mechanism for every
Team Membership created within the Evolution
Hub Operating System.

The Repository shall preserve:

• Membership identity.

• Member references.

• Team references.

• Membership lifecycle.

• Membership status.

• Historical membership records.

The Team Membership Repository is the
constitutional memory of Team Membership.

It preserves constitutional truth without
interpreting constitutional truth.

Business decisions, governance decisions, and
workflow execution remain outside the scope of
the Repository and belong to their respective
constitutional Services.

The Repository exists solely to ensure that
every Team Membership remains persistent,
traceable, auditable, and constitutionally
consistent throughout its entire lifecycle.
