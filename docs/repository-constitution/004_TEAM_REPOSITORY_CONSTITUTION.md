PURPOSE

The Team Repository exists as the
constitutional persistence layer responsible
for maintaining approved Team-related
platform state within the Evolution Hub
Operating System.

It provides reliable storage and retrieval
of Team information while remaining aligned
with the EHOS constitutional order.

==========================================================

CONSTITUTIONAL ROLE

The Team Repository serves as the persistence
foundation for Team Services.

Its role is to:

• Store Team-related data.

• Retrieve authorized Team information.

• Maintain Team data integrity.

• Support approved Team operations.

• Preserve historical Team records.

==========================================================

REPOSITORY PRINCIPLE

The Team Repository is a servant of the
platform architecture.

It does not own constitutional authority.

It does not create platform rules.

It does not determine participant rights.

It does not grant capabilities.

==========================================================

RESPONSIBILITY BOUNDARY

The Team Repository is responsible for:

• Data persistence.

• Data retrieval.

• Data consistency.

• Data organization.

• Persistence reliability.

==========================================================

CAPABILITIES

The Team Repository has the capability to:

• Store Team entities.

• Retrieve Team entities.

• Update approved Team records.

• Remove approved Team records according
  to system rules.

• Maintain Team relationships.

• Support authorized queries.

• Preserve Team history where required.

==========================================================

LIABILITIES

The Team Repository shall not:

• Execute business logic.

• Make governance decisions.

• Interpret constitutional rules.

• Evaluate member contribution.

• Grant Evolution Capacity.

• Manage Team permissions independently.

• Replace Team Services.

• Replace Platform Operations.

==========================================================

CONSTITUTIONAL CONSTRAINT

The Team Repository remembers.

The Team Repository does not decide.

Decisions belong to authorized services and
constitutional offices.

PURPOSE

The Team Repository role is to provide the
authorized persistence contract through
which Team Services interact with stored
Team information.

It exists between the Service Layer and the
Database Layer while preserving the
separation of responsibilities defined by
the EHOS Constitution.

==========================================================

EXECUTION POSITION

The Team Repository operates within the
following execution chain:

==========================================================

Constitution

        ↓

Platform Offices

        ↓

Team Services

        ↓

Team Repository

        ↓

ORM

        ↓

Database

==========================================================

REPOSITORY ROLE

The Team Repository shall:

• Provide controlled access to Team data.

• Translate service requirements into
  persistence operations.

• Protect database interaction boundaries.

• Maintain consistency between services
  and stored information.

==========================================================

SERVICE RELATIONSHIP

Team Services are responsible for:

• Business logic.

• Workflow decisions.

• Team operations.

• Authorization decisions.

The Team Repository supports Team Services
by providing persistence capability.

==========================================================

DATABASE RELATIONSHIP

The Database is responsible for:

• Physical data storage.

• Data persistence.

• Storage infrastructure.

The Team Repository is responsible for:

• Access patterns.

• Data retrieval contracts.

• Persistence abstraction.

==========================================================

ORM RELATIONSHIP

The ORM layer provides:

• Entity mapping.

• Database communication.

• Query translation.

The Team Repository uses the ORM but does
not become the ORM.

==========================================================

PRIMARY OFFICE

Platform Operations Office

The Team Repository supports operational
execution of Team-related services.

----------------------------------------------------------

GOVERNANCE ALIGNMENT

Platform Governance Office ensures:

• Constitutional compliance.

• Boundary protection.

• Proper authority separation.

----------------------------------------------------------

CAPACITY ALIGNMENT

Evolution Capacity Office ensures:

• Capability rules remain external.

• Progression decisions remain separated.

----------------------------------------------------------

STEWARDSHIP ALIGNMENT

Visionary Stewardship Chamber ensures:

• Team evolution remains aligned with EHOS
  vision.

==========================================================

CAPABILITIES

• Act as the persistence contract for Team
  services.

• Provide stable data access patterns.

• Support future implementation layers.

• Maintain architectural consistency.

==========================================================

LIABILITIES

The Team Repository shall not:

• Become a service.

• Become an authority layer.

• Contain business decisions.

• Contain governance rules.

• Contain capacity evaluation.

PURPOSE

The Team Repository scope defines the exact
boundary of data, entities, and persistence
responsibilities that belong to the Team
Repository.

This scope prevents unauthorized expansion
of repository responsibility.

==========================================================

IN SCOPE

The Team Repository shall manage persistence
for approved Team-related entities.

These may include:

----------------------------------------------------------

TEAM ENTITY

Purpose:

Represents a collaborative Team structure
within the Evolution Hub Operating System.

Stored Information:

• Team identity.

• Team metadata.

• Team status.

• Team creation information.

• Team lifecycle information.

----------------------------------------------------------

TEAM MEMBERSHIP ENTITY

Purpose:

Represents the relationship between
participants and Teams.

Stored Information:

• Team membership records.

• Membership status.

• Membership dates.

• Membership relationships.

----------------------------------------------------------

TEAM INVITATION ENTITY

Purpose:

Represents approved requests to join Teams.

Stored Information:

• Invitation records.

• Invitation status.

• Sender information reference.

• Recipient information reference.

----------------------------------------------------------

TEAM ROLE ENTITY

Purpose:

Represents Team-level role definitions.

Stored Information:

• Role identity.

• Role description.

• Role assignment references.

==========================================================

OUT OF SCOPE

The Team Repository shall not manage:

----------------------------------------------------------

GOVERNANCE DATA

Owned by:

Platform Governance Office

Examples:

• Constitutional rules.

• Policy interpretation.

• Compliance decisions.

----------------------------------------------------------

CAPACITY DATA

Owned by:

Evolution Capacity Office

Examples:

• Evolution Stars.

• Capacity levels.

• Earned privileges.

• Progression decisions.

----------------------------------------------------------

COMMERCE DATA

Owned by:

Platform Commerce Office

Examples:

• Transactions.

• Payments.

• Marketplace records.

----------------------------------------------------------

INDUSTRY DATA

Owned by:

Platform Industry Office

Examples:

• Industry structures.

• Sector systems.

• Industry opportunities.

----------------------------------------------------------

VISIONARY DATA

Owned by:

Visionary Stewardship Chamber

Examples:

• Vision records.

• Strategic guidance.

• Foundational philosophy.

==========================================================

SCOPE BOUNDARY PRINCIPLE

The Team Repository stores Team state.

It does not store every piece of information
connected to a Team participant.

Related information remains owned by its
proper constitutional domain.

==========================================================

CAPABILITIES

The Team Repository has the capability to:

• Persist Team domain entities.

• Maintain Team relationships.

• Support Team lifecycle storage.

• Support authorized Team queries.

• Prepare data structures for future
  implementation.

==========================================================

LIABILITIES

The Team Repository shall not:

• Become a universal user repository.

• Become a governance repository.

• Become a capacity repository.

• Become a commerce repository.

• Become an intelligence decision system.

==========================================================

CONSTITUTIONAL CONSTRAINT

A relationship does not transfer ownership.

A Team may contain participants.

The Team Repository does not own the
participant's entire identity.

PURPOSE

The Repository Operations section defines

authorized persistence responsibilities.

not business authority.

==========================================================

OPERATIONAL PRINCIPLE

The Team Repository performs persistence
operations only when requested by
authorized services.

The Repository does not initiate business
actions independently.

==========================================================

SERVICE REQUEST FLOW

The Team Repository operates through:

==========================================================

Team Service

        ↓

Repository Contract

        ↓

Team Repository

        ↓

ORM Layer

        ↓

Database

==========================================================

TEAM CREATION OPERATION

Purpose:

Persist an approved Team creation request.

Process:

1. Team Service validates creation rules.

2. Team Service sends approved data.

3. Repository stores Team record.

4. Database confirms persistence.

Repository Responsibility:

• Store data.

• Maintain integrity.

• Return persistence result.

Repository Restriction:

The Repository does not decide whether the
Team should exist.

==========================================================

TEAM MEMBERSHIP OPERATION

Purpose:

Maintain Team membership records.

Process:

1. Authorized service validates membership.

2. Repository receives approved request.

3. Repository updates membership state.

Repository Responsibility:

• Store membership relationship.

• Maintain consistency.

Repository Restriction:

The Repository does not approve membership.

==========================================================

TEAM ROLE OPERATION

Purpose:

Maintain Team role information.

Process:

1. Service determines role assignment.

2. Repository persists role relationship.

Repository Responsibility:

• Store role information.

Repository Restriction:

The Repository does not define authority.

==========================================================

TEAM RETRIEVAL OPERATION

Purpose:

Provide Team information to authorized
services.

Repository Responsibility:

• Retrieve requested records.

• Maintain query consistency.

Repository Restriction:

The Repository does not decide who may
access information.

==========================================================

TRANSACTION OPERATIONS

The Team Repository shall support:

• Consistent data operations.

• Atomic persistence actions.

• Data integrity protection.

• Recovery from failed operations.

==========================================================

ERROR HANDLING PRINCIPLE

Repository errors shall communicate:

• Persistence failure.

• Data conflict.

• Invalid storage request.

Repository errors shall not:

• Make business decisions.

• Resolve governance conflicts.

• Modify authority rules.

==========================================================

AUDIT OPERATIONS

Where required, the Repository may record:

• Creation timestamps.

• Update timestamps.

• Change history.

• Record lifecycle events.

==========================================================

CAPABILITIES

Repository Operations have the capability
to:

• Execute reliable persistence workflows.

• Maintain data consistency.

• Support service execution.

• Protect stored Team information.

==========================================================

LIABILITIES

Repository Operations shall not:

• Execute user-facing workflows.

• Replace Team Services.

• Create authorization logic.

• Control participant actions.

==========================================================

Repositories execute instructions.

Services create actions.

Offices hold responsibility.

The Constitution defines authority.

PURPOSE

The Persistence Rules define the standards
that govern how the Team Repository stores,
maintains, protects, and retrieves Team
data within the Evolution Hub Operating
System.

Persistence shall preserve truth.

Persistence shall not create authority.

==========================================================

RULE 1

DATA OWNERSHIP RULE

Every stored record shall belong to a
defined constitutional domain.

The Team Repository shall only persist
Team domain information.

==========================================================

RULE 2

DATA INTEGRITY RULE

The Team Repository shall protect:

• Valid records.

• Valid relationships.

• Consistent state.

• Reliable persistence.

Invalid or corrupted state shall not become
accepted platform truth.

==========================================================

RULE 3

SOURCE OF TRUTH RULE

The Team Repository shall serve as the
authorized persistence source for approved
Team entities.

It shall not become the source of truth for:

• Governance.

• Capacity.

• Commerce.

• Industry.

• Stewardship.

==========================================================

RULE 4

HISTORICAL RECORD RULE

Where required, the Team Repository shall
preserve:

• Creation history.

• Modification history.

• Relationship history.

• Lifecycle events.

Historical records exist for accountability
and learning.

==========================================================

RULE 5

DATA ACCESS RULE

Access to Team data shall occur through
authorized service contracts.

Direct uncontrolled access is prohibited.

==========================================================

RULE 6

PRIVACY RULE

The Team Repository shall protect stored
participant-related Team information.

It shall respect:

• Privacy principles.

• Authorized access boundaries.

• Data protection requirements.

==========================================================

RULE 7

MIGRATION RULE

All Team Repository schema changes shall:

• Be documented.

• Be version controlled.

• Preserve data integrity.

• Support controlled migration.

==========================================================

RULE 8

DELETION RULE

Deletion of Team information shall follow
approved lifecycle policies.

Deletion shall not occur outside authorized
workflows.

==========================================================

RULE 9

BACKUP AND RECOVERY RULE

The Team Repository shall support:

• Backup procedures.

• Recovery procedures.

• Data restoration processes.

==========================================================

RULE 10

CONSISTENCY RULE

Repository operations shall preserve
consistent Team state.

Partial or invalid state shall not be
considered complete.

==========================================================

CAPABILITIES

Persistence Rules provide the capability to:

• Protect Team information.

• Maintain reliable records.

• Support long-term data integrity.

• Enable safe evolution.

==========================================================

LIABILITIES

Persistence Rules shall not:

• Define business decisions.

• Create participant authority.

• Grant permissions.

• Replace Governance.

==========================================================

CONSTITUTIONAL CONSTRAINT

The Repository preserves memory.

The Repository does not define reality.

Reality is defined by constitutional
services and responsible offices.
