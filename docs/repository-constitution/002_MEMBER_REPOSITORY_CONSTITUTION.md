PART I

CONSTITUTIONAL PURPOSE
==========================================================

The Member Repository exists to
constitutionally preserve every Member of the
Evolution Hub Operating System.

Its purpose is to provide a trusted,
consistent, and auditable constitutional
persistence mechanism for Member records
throughout their lawful lifecycle.

The Repository preserves:

• Member identity.

• Constitutional Member profile.

• Constitutional Member status.

• Membership history.

• Constitutional relationships owned by the
  Member Domain.

The Member Repository serves as the permanent
constitutional memory of every Member.

It preserves constitutional truth but does
not interpret constitutional truth.

Business rules, governance decisions,
authorization, and workflow execution remain
the responsibility of their respective
constitutional Services.

The Repository exists solely to ensure that
every constitutional Member remains
persistent, traceable, auditable, and
constitutionally consistent throughout the
entire lifecycle of the Evolution Hub
Operating System.

the constitutional memory of every Member and
nothing more.
