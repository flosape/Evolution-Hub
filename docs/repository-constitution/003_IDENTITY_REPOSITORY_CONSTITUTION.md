PART I

CONSTITUTIONAL PURPOSE
==========================================================

The Identity Repository exists to
constitutionally preserve the unique
identity of every constitutional Member
within the Evolution Hub Operating System.

Its purpose is to provide a trusted,
consistent, immutable, and auditable
constitutional persistence mechanism for
Identity records throughout their lawful
lifecycle.

The Repository preserves:

• Constitutional Identity.

• Identity identifiers.

• Identity lifecycle.

• Identity history.

• Constitutional identity integrity.

The Identity Repository serves as the
constitutional source of identity truth for
the entire Evolution Hub Operating System.

It preserves constitutional truth without
interpreting constitutional truth.

Authentication, authorization, governance,
and workflow execution remain the
responsibility of their respective
constitutional Services.

The Repository exists solely to ensure that
every constitutional Identity remains
persistent, immutable, traceable,
auditable, and constitutionally consistent
throughout the lifecycle of the Evolution
Hub Operating System.

as the constitutional memory of every
