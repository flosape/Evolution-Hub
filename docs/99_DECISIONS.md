# Evolution Hub Architecture Decision Register (ADR)

This document records the major architectural and engineering decisions that govern the Evolution Hub platform.

---

## ADR-001

**Decision:** Next.js is the official frontend framework.

**Status:** Approved

---

## ADR-002

**Decision:** The platform is organized into 12 Platform Domains.

**Status:** Approved

---

## ADR-003

**Decision:** Platform Infrastructure is separate from Platform Domains.

**Status:** Approved

---

## ADR-004

**Decision:** User experience is organized by Opportunity, not by modules.

**Status:** Approved

---

## ADR-005

**Decision:** Preserve and migrate the original Evolution Hub prototype into the official Next.js platform.

**Status:** Approved

---

## ADR-006

**Decision:** Evolution Hub is an Intelligence Platform, not merely a business application.

**Status:** Approved

---

## ADR-007

**Decision:** The Four Evolution Engines form the strategic capability layer of the platform.

**Status:** Approved

### The Four Evolution Engines

- 🧠 Intelligence Engine
- 🏢 Business Engine
- 💰 Commerce Engine
- 🌍 Industry Engine

---

## ADR-008

**Decision:** The 12 Platform Domains are engineering implementation domains and are not the primary user navigation model.

**Status:** Approved

---

## ADR-009

**Decision:** Platform Services are shared capabilities used across every domain.

**Status:** Approved

---

## ADR-010

**Decision:** Platform architecture is domain-driven while the product experience is opportunity-driven.

**Status:** Approved

---

## ADR-011

**Decision:** Every engineering decision must strengthen the platform, align with the architecture, scale for the future, and simplify the user experience.

**Status:** Approved

---

## ADR-012

**Decision:** Major architectural decisions must be documented before implementation.

**Status:** Approved

---
