CONSTITUTIONAL PURPOSE

==========================================================

Industry ecosystem and defines the
rights, services, obligations, and
benefits associated with that
participation.

preserves consistency, accountability,
and long-term continuity for every
Organization operating within an
Industry.

MILESTONE 012.3

INDUSTRY SUBSCRIPTION DEFINITION

==========================================================

An Industry Subscription is the
constitutional relationship that connects
an Organization to a specific Industry
within the Evolution Hub Operating
System.

It records the Organization's recognized
participation, subscription status,
service eligibility, obligations, and
historical association with that
Industry.

Every Industry Subscription shall:

• Belong to one constitutional
  Organization.

• Reference one constitutional Industry.

• Maintain one constitutional
  subscription record.

• Preserve its own lifecycle.

• Maintain its own operational status.

• Preserve constitutional audit history.

An Industry Subscription is not:

• An Industry.

• An Organization.

• An Organization Membership.

• A Workspace.

• A Project.

• A Task.

It exists solely to establish and govern
constitutional participation between an
Organization and an Industry.

MILESTONE 012.5

INDUSTRY SUBSCRIPTION RELATIONSHIPS

==========================================================

The Industry Subscription Domain maintains
the following constitutional relationships.

ORGANIZATION

One Organization may have one or more
Industry Subscriptions where permitted
by constitutional rules.

Each Industry Subscription shall belong
to one Organization.

INDUSTRY

One Industry may have many Industry
Subscriptions.

Each Industry Subscription shall reference
one Industry.

ORGANIZATION MEMBERSHIP

Organization Membership determines who
may represent an Organization.

Industry Subscription determines the
Organization's participation within an
Industry.

WORKSPACE

Workspaces may inherit Industry
Subscription context from their parent
Organization but shall not own Industry
Subscriptions.

PROJECT

Projects may inherit Industry
Subscription context for analytics,
benchmarking, and AI assistance.

TASK

Tasks may inherit Industry context
through:

Organization

→ Industry Subscription

→ Industry

for execution intelligence,
benchmarking, reporting, and automation.

EVOLUTION ARSENAL

The Evolution Arsenal may use Industry
Subscription context to provide:

• Industry-specific intelligence.

• Subscription services.

• AI recommendations.

• Analytics.

• Benchmarking.

• Automation opportunities.

These relationships ensure that Industry
participation remains governed through
one constitutional source of truth.

==========================================================
MILESTONE 012.7

LIFECYCLE

==========================================================

Every constitutional Industry Subscription
progresses through the following
lifecycle states.

REQUESTED

The Organization has requested
participation within an Industry, but
approval has not yet been completed.

PENDING

The subscription is undergoing review,
validation, or activation processing.

ACTIVE

The Industry Subscription is approved
and the Organization has recognized
participation within the Industry.

SUSPENDED

The subscription is temporarily paused
while preserving historical records and
relationship continuity.

EXPIRED

The subscription period has ended and
requires renewal or reactivation.

CANCELLED

The Organization or authorized authority
has ended the subscription.

TERMINATED

The subscription has been permanently
ended according to constitutional rules.

Every lifecycle transition shall be:

• Authorized.

• Traceable.

• Auditable.

• Constitutionally recorded.

Historical Industry Subscription records
shall remain preserved after expiration,
cancellation, or termination.

STATUS MODEL

==========================================================

Every constitutional Industry Subscription
shall maintain one operational status at
any given time.

the Organization has access to approved
Industry participation benefits.

INACTIVE

The subscription exists constitutionally
but is not currently available for
active participation.

PENDING_APPROVAL

The subscription is awaiting validation
or authorization before activation.

RESTRICTED

The subscription is active but limited by
constitutional, regulatory, service, or
administrative conditions.

SUSPENDED

The subscription is temporarily disabled
while preserving its historical record.

ARCHIVED

The subscription is preserved for
historical reference and analytics only.

The operational status of an Industry
Subscription shall never:

• Change ownership.

• Modify Industry identity.

• Modify Organization identity.

• Delete constitutional history.

All status changes shall be:

• Authorized.

• Auditable.

• Traceable.

MILESTONE 012.10

CONSTITUTIONAL CONSTRAINTS

==========================================================

The Industry Subscription Domain shall
operate under the following constitutional
constraints.

• Every Industry Subscription shall have
  one unique Subscription Identity.

• Every Industry Subscription shall belong
  to one Organization.

• Every Industry Subscription shall
  reference one Industry.

• Every Industry Subscription shall
  maintain its own lifecycle.

• Every Industry Subscription shall
  maintain its own operational status.

• Every Industry Subscription shall
  preserve complete audit history.

• Industry Subscription shall never
  create or modify Industry identity.

• Industry Subscription shall never
  create or modify Organization identity.

• Industry Subscription shall never become
  an ownership authority.

• Industry Subscription shall never replace
  Organization Membership.

• Industry Subscription shall never manage
  execution resources such as Projects or
  Tasks.

• Historical subscription records shall
  remain preserved after expiration,
  cancellation, or termination.

No implementation shall violate these
constitutional constraints.
