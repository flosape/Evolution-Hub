MILESTONE 007.3

OPPORTUNITY DEFINITION
==========================================================

An Opportunity is a constitutional record
representing a lawful possibility to
create, capture, or realize measurable
value within the Evolution Hub Operating
System.

Every Opportunity exists to evaluate and
manage potential value resulting from
constitutional execution.

Every Opportunity shall:

• Possess one unique Opportunity
  identity.

• Belong to one constitutional
  Workspace.

• Be traceable to its constitutional
  source.

• Maintain measurable value objectives.

• Preserve its own lifecycle and history.

An Opportunity is not a Project.

An Opportunity is not a Workspace.

An Opportunity represents the measurable
value that may result from constitutional
execution.

STATUS MODEL
==========================================================

The Opportunity Domain recognizes the
following constitutional operational
status values.

IDENTIFIED

The Opportunity has been constitutionally
recorded and awaits evaluation.

EVALUATING

The Opportunity is undergoing lawful
assessment for value, feasibility, risk,
and strategic alignment.

APPROVED

The Opportunity has been constitutionally
approved for pursuit.

ACTIVE

The Opportunity is actively progressing
towards measurable value realization.

REALIZED

The Opportunity has successfully produced
its intended constitutional value.

REJECTED

The Opportunity has been lawfully
declined following constitutional
evaluation.

ARCHIVED

The Opportunity is permanently preserved
as part of the constitutional history of
Evolution Hub.

An Opportunity status shall never alter
its constitutional identity.

Every status transition shall be fully
auditable and constitutionally
traceable.
