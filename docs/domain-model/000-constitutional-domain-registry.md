  history.

• A Team.

• A Team Membership.

• A Workspace.

• A Project.

constitutional participation within an
Organization.

==========================================================
MILESTONE 010.7

LIFECYCLE

==========================================================

Every constitutional Organization
Membership follows a controlled lifecycle.

LIFECYCLE STAGES

• Invited

• Pending Acceptance

• Active

• Suspended

• Reactivated

• Resigned

• Removed

• Archived

----------------------------------------------------------

INVITED

An invitation has been issued but has not
yet been accepted.

----------------------------------------------------------

PENDING ACCEPTANCE

The invitation has been received and is
awaiting constitutional acceptance.

----------------------------------------------------------

ACTIVE

The member is constitutionally recognized
and may participate according to their
authorized role.

----------------------------------------------------------

SUSPENDED

Participation is temporarily suspended
while the constitutional membership
record remains intact.

----------------------------------------------------------

REACTIVATED

A previously suspended membership has
been constitutionally restored.

----------------------------------------------------------

RESIGNED

The member has voluntarily ended their
constitutional participation.

----------------------------------------------------------

REMOVED

The Organization has lawfully terminated
the membership.

----------------------------------------------------------

ARCHIVED

The membership is permanently preserved
as part of the Organization's
constitutional history.

==========================================================

CONSTITUTIONAL LIFECYCLE RULES

• Every lifecycle transition shall remain
  fully auditable.

• A Resigned or Removed membership shall
  never return directly to Active.

• Archived memberships are immutable
  constitutional records.

• Every Organization Membership shall
  ultimately terminate in one of the
  following states:

  • Resigned
  • Removed
  • Archived
