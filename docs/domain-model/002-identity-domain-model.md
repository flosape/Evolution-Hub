MILESTONE 002.4

IDENTITY STRUCTURE
==========================================================

Every constitutional identity within
Evolution Hub is composed of immutable
and mutable identity components.

IMMUTABLE COMPONENTS

• Constitutional Identity
• Constitutional Citizen Number
• Platform Identity UUID
• Date of Identity Creation

These components establish the permanent
constitutional existence of a User and
shall never be reassigned or transferred.

MUTABLE COMPONENTS

• Display Name
• Profile Information
• Contact Information
• Authentication Credentials
• Security Configuration

These components may evolve while the
constitutional identity remains unchanged.

Identity shall remain unique across the
entire Evolution Hub Operating System.

No two Users shall share the same
constitutional identity.

Authentication mechanisms may change.

Identity shall not.

MILESTONE 002.8

STATUS MODEL
==========================================================

The Identity Domain recognizes the
following constitutional status values.

CREATED

A constitutional identity has been
established but has not yet entered the
verification process.

PENDING VERIFICATION

Identity exists and awaits successful
verification before platform participation.

ACTIVE

Identity is constitutionally recognized
and may participate in the Evolution Hub
Operating System.

SUSPENDED

Identity participation is temporarily
restricted through constitutional
governance.

LOCKED

Identity is temporarily protected due to
security or constitutional enforcement.

ARCHIVED

Identity is permanently retired from
active participation while remaining part
of the constitutional history of the
platform.

No status shall alter or destroy the
constitutional identity itself.
