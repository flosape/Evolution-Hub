MILESTONE 004.1

CONSTITUTIONAL PURPOSE
==========================================================

The Team Membership Domain exists to
establish the lawful constitutional
relationship between a User and a Team.

Team Membership authorizes a
constitutional User to participate in the
activities of a Team without transferring
or altering the User's constitutional
identity.

Team Membership preserves the integrity
of collaboration by defining how Users
join, serve within, and leave Teams.

A Team Membership is a constitutional
relationship aggregate.

It exists independently from both the
User and the Team while permanently
linking them through lawful
participation.

Team Membership exists to ensure that all
Team collaboration is constitutional,
traceable, auditable, and governed by the
Evolution Hub Operating System.

MILESTONE 004.7

LIFECYCLE
==========================================================

Every constitutional Team Membership
follows a controlled lifecycle governed
by the Evolution Hub Constitution.

LIFECYCLE STAGES

• Requested
• Pending Approval
• Active
• Suspended
• Voluntarily Left
• Removed
• Archived

REQUESTED

A constitutional User voluntarily submits
a request to join a constitutional Team.

PENDING APPROVAL

The request awaits review by the
authorized Team leadership or
constitutional governance mechanism.

ACTIVE

The User becomes an active constitutional
member of the Team and may lawfully
participate in Team execution.

SUSPENDED

Membership is temporarily restricted
through constitutional governance while
preserving historical records.

VOLUNTARILY LEFT

The User voluntarily exits the Team.

The membership slot immediately becomes
available for another constitutional User.

REMOVED

Membership is terminated through lawful
constitutional governance.

ARCHIVED

The membership record becomes part of the
permanent constitutional history of
Evolution Hub.

• Every Team operates within a
  constitutional membership capacity.

• When Team capacity is reached, no new
  active memberships shall be created
  until a constitutional vacancy exists.

• Every lifecycle transition shall be
  fully auditable.
