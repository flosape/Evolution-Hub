unit of collaboration within the
Evolution Hub Operating System.

identity.

authority.

The Team operates under the Constitution
of Evolution Hub while contributing to
the continuous evolution of the platform.

MILESTONE 003.3

TEAM DEFINITION
==========================================================

A Team is a constitutional collaboration
aggregate composed of two or more
constitutional Users working together
under a shared objective.

A Team exists independently of any
individual member while preserving the
constitutional identity of every User.

Every Team shall:

• Possess a unique Team identity.

• Operate within one constitutional
  Workspace.

• Maintain its own lifecycle.

• Maintain its own membership.

• Produce work collectively.

A Team may own Projects and create
Opportunities through lawful execution.

A Team cannot exist without at least one
constitutional User.

A Team is not an Organization.

A Team becomes eligible for
Organizational recognition only through
constitutional governance and sustained
value creation.

MILESTONE 003.7

LIFECYCLE
==========================================================

Every constitutional Team follows a
controlled lifecycle governed by the
Evolution Hub Constitution.

LIFECYCLE STATES

• Created
• Active
• Growing
• Mature
• Suspended
• Archived

CREATED

The Team has been constitutionally
established but has not yet begun active
collaboration.

ACTIVE

The Team is actively collaborating and
executing work within its assigned
Workspace.

GROWING

The Team is expanding its membership,
capabilities, and execution capacity.

MATURE

The Team has demonstrated sustained
collaboration, consistent execution, and
stable operational performance.

SUSPENDED

Team activities are temporarily restricted
through constitutional governance.

ARCHIVED

The Team has concluded active execution
and is preserved as part of the permanent
constitutional history of Evolution Hub.

A Team may transition between lifecycle
states only through authorized
constitutional services.

Lifecycle changes shall be fully auditable
and traceable.
