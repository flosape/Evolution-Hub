ORGANIZATION DEFINITION
==========================================================

An Organization is the constitutional
institution responsible for managing
resources, providing strategic direction,
and enabling lawful execution within the
Evolution Hub Operating System.

Every Organization exists to coordinate
constitutional Workspaces, Teams,
Projects, Opportunities, and other
managed resources in pursuit of its
mission and objectives.

Every Organization shall:

• Possess one unique Organization
  identity.

• Manage one or more constitutional
  Workspaces.

• Establish strategic direction.

• Manage constitutional resources.

• Preserve institutional continuity.

• Maintain its own constitutional
  lifecycle and history.

An Organization is not a Workspace.

An Organization is not a Team.

An Organization provides constitutional
resource management and strategic
coordination for lawful execution.

MILESTONE 008.7

LIFECYCLE
==========================================================

Every constitutional Organization follows
a controlled lifecycle governed by the
Evolution Hub Constitution.

LIFECYCLE STAGES

• Established
• Active
• Growing
• Mature
• Suspended
• Dissolved
• Archived

ESTABLISHED

The Organization has been
constitutionally created but has not yet
commenced full operations.

ACTIVE

The Organization is managing
constitutional resources and supporting
lawful execution.

GROWING

The Organization is expanding its
managed Workspaces, Teams, Projects,
resources, and operational capacity.

MATURE

The Organization has achieved stable,
sustainable, and effective constitutional
operations.

SUSPENDED

Organizational operations are temporarily
restricted while preserving all
constitutional records.

DISSOLVED

The Organization has lawfully concluded
its operations in accordance with the
Constitution.

ARCHIVED

The Organization is permanently preserved
as part of the constitutional history of
Evolution Hub.

==========================================================

CONSTITUTIONAL LIFECYCLE RULES

• Every lifecycle transition shall remain
  fully auditable.

• A Dissolved Organization shall not
  return to Active status.

• Archived Organizations remain immutable
  constitutional records.
