CONSTITUTIONAL PURPOSE

==========================================================

The Industry Domain exists to classify,
standardize, and govern the economic
sectors in which constitutional
Organizations operate.

It provides a unified industry framework
that enables intelligent organization,
benchmarking, analytics, compliance,
collaboration, and ecosystem growth.

The Industry Domain establishes a common
language for economic activities across
the Evolution Hub Operating System,
ensuring that Organizations operating in
similar sectors can be understood,
compared, supported, and empowered
consistently.

MILESTONE 011.3

INDUSTRY DEFINITION

==========================================================

An Industry is a constitutional economic
classification that groups Organizations
operating within the same or closely
related sectors of economic activity.

An Industry provides the shared context
through which Organizations can be:

• Classified.

• Compared.

• Benchmarked.

• Analyzed.

• Supported.

• Connected.

Every constitutional Industry shall:

• Possess one unique constitutional
  Industry Identity.

• Maintain one constitutional Industry
  Profile.

• Support multiple Organizations.

• Preserve its own lifecycle.

• Maintain its own status.

• Preserve its constitutional audit
  history.

An Industry is not:

• An Organization.

• A Workspace.

• A Project.

• A Team.

• A Task.

An Industry exists solely to provide
constitutional classification and
ecosystem context for Organizations.

INDUSTRY RELATIONSHIPS

==========================================================

The Industry Domain maintains the
following constitutional relationships.

ORGANIZATION

One Industry may classify many
Organizations.

Each constitutional Organization shall
belong to one constitutional Industry,
unless future constitutional laws permit
multiple industry classifications.

OPPORTUNITY

Industries provide the economic context
through which Opportunities are
categorized, discovered, and evaluated.

TASK

Tasks may inherit Industry context through
their parent Organization for analytics,
automation, benchmarking, reporting, and
AI orchestration.

WORKSPACE

Workspaces inherit Industry context from
their parent Organization and shall not
maintain independent Industry ownership.

PROJECT

Projects inherit Industry context from
their parent Workspace and Organization.

EVOLUTION ARSENAL

The Evolution Arsenal may utilize
Industry context to deliver:

• Industry-specific intelligence.

• AI recommendations.

• Benchmarking.

• Regulatory guidance.

• Analytics.

• Workflow optimization.

• Knowledge discovery.

These relationships preserve one
constitutional source of Industry truth
throughout the Evolution Hub Operating
System.

MILESTONE 011.7

LIFECYCLE

==========================================================

Every constitutional Industry progresses
through the following lifecycle.

PROPOSED

The Industry has been identified for
constitutional consideration but has not
yet been approved.

ACTIVE

The Industry is constitutionally approved
and available for Organization
classification and ecosystem operations.

SUSPENDED

The Industry is temporarily unavailable
for new classifications while existing
Organizations retain their historical
association.

MERGED

The Industry has been constitutionally
merged into another Industry.

Historical records remain preserved.

RETIRED

The Industry is permanently retired from
active constitutional use.

Historical relationships remain immutable
for audit, reporting, and analytics.

Every Industry transition shall be:

• Authorized.

• Auditable.

• Traceable.

• Constitutionally recorded.
